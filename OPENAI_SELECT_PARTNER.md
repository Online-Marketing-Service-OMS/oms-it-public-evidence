## Scope boundary

The organization-level Select Partner status must not automatically be extended to:

- BD AI;
- OMS Nexus;
- customer projects;
- client organizations;
- individual applications;
- AI systems;
- courses or training products;
- publications;
- websites or services.

No such product-level or project-level OpenAI approval, certification, sponsorship, authorization, or endorsement should be inferred unless separate authoritative evidence supports that exact claim.

## Independent verification note

This repository is controlled by OMS IT and therefore functions as a **public evidence record**, not as an OpenAI-controlled directory.

OpenAI Partner Marketing has separately clarified that new Select partners are not automatically included in the public Partner Locator. If OMS IT later becomes eligible for and receives an OpenAI-hosted public partner profile, that OpenAI-controlled page should be treated as the strongest independent public verification source.
