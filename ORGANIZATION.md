## OpenAI relationship wording

The approved public relationship wording used in this repository is:

> **OMS IT is an OpenAI Select Partner.**

This wording refers to **OMS IT at the organization level**.

It must not be interpreted as a claim that OpenAI has separately approved, certified, endorsed, sponsored, authorized, or guaranteed any OMS IT product, BD AI, OMS Nexus, customer project, website, course, application, publication, or deliverable.

## Relationship clarity

Technology contribution, ownership, operation, partnership, certification, and endorsement are separate concepts. Public claims should state only the relationship supported by evidence.
