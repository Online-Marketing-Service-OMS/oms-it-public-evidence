# OPN-06 — Partner Marketing Press-Release Approval

**Status:** Source not yet supplied for review in this evidence pack.

This record is reserved for the OpenAI Partner Marketing communication that reportedly reviewed and approved a specific OMS IT press-release copy.

When the source is supplied, the public record should capture only:

- sender domain and role, where appropriate to disclose;
- date;
- subject/context;
- the exact scope of what was approved;
- any external-sharing restrictions;
- a SHA-256 hash of the reviewed source file or exported message.

Approval of a specific press-release copy should not be described as broader product approval, certification, endorsement, or authorization beyond the communication's actual scope.
