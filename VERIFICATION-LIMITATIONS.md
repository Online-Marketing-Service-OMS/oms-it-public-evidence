# Verification Limitations

**Last reviewed:** 11 October 2026

## What is supported

The evidence currently published in this repository supports the specific public claim that **Online Marketing Service (OMS IT) holds organization-level OpenAI Select Partner status**.

The evidence set includes public-safe summaries of issuer-originated communications and first-party authenticated Partner Portal records. See [EVIDENCE_INDEX.md](./EVIDENCE_INDEX.md).

## What is not implied

The organization-level status does not automatically establish:

- OpenAI approval, certification, endorsement, sponsorship, authorization, or operation of an OMS IT product or service;
- OpenAI approval or operation of BD AI;
- certification of an individual application, course, publication, website, or customer project;
- guaranteed customer outcomes;
- government affiliation;
- ownership of a third-party or customer organization.

## Independent verification

OMS IT's GitHub repositories and website are organization-controlled sources. They can preserve evidence and make claims auditable, but they are not independent verification by OpenAI.

When an OpenAI-controlled public record is available for the relevant claim or tier, it should be treated as the strongest public verification source. Authenticated Partner Portal evidence and issuer-originated documents are retained or summarized as supporting evidence subject to confidentiality and publication restrictions.

## Privacy and security

Do not publish passwords, API keys, access tokens, session information, private Partner Portal data, customer-confidential data, unnecessary personal information, or restricted documents. Redact or summarize evidence where needed.
